# PERFORM COMPUTER REPAIR AND MAINTENANCE

## Description

Computer repair and maintenance is the process of inspecting, diagnosing, repairing, upgrading, and maintaining computer hardware and software to ensure reliable, efficient, and secure operation. It involves identifying faults, replacing defective components, testing system performance, and carrying out preventive maintenance to minimize downtime and extend the lifespan of computer systems.
